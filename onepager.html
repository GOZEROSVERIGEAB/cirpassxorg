CIRPASSX
Circular Product Passport Exchange Standard
Open Source · MIT License · v1.0

The Universal Language for Secondhand Products

CIRPASSX is an open data standard enabling seamless exchange of product information between reuse actors, marketplaces, and circular economy platforms. Built for EU Digital Product Passport compliance and the Right to Repair directive.

Key Features

  • JSON-LD format with Schema.org vocabulary
  • W3C Verifiable Credentials envelope
  • Standardized condition grading (A-F)
  • Lifecycle event tracking (EPCIS 2.0)
  • Repairability scoring (EN45554)
  • Spare parts information
  • Material composition & recycled content
  • GS1 Digital Link identifiers
