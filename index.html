Open Standard for Circular Economy

CIRPASSX is an open data standard enabling seamless exchange of secondhand product information between reuse actors, marketplaces, and buyers across Europe. Built for compliance with EU regulations and designed for the future of sustainable commerce.

Standardized Exchange

One format for all systems. Share product data between any platform using JSON-LD and Schema.org vocabulary for maximum interoperability.

EU Compliant

Designed for EU Digital Product Passport (DPP) requirements under ESPR and full support for the Right to Repair directive.

Lifecycle Tracking

Track products from manufacturing through repair, salvage, and resale with EPCIS 2.0 compatible event history.

Standards Compatibility

EU DPP UNTP Schema.org GS1 Digital Link EPCIS 2.0 EN45554 W3C VC

Required Fields

Required fields for CIRPASSX products
Field Description
identifier CIRPASSX_ID (UUID) - unique product identifier
name Product name (max 200 characters)
category Classification code (UNSPSC, ECLASS, or other)
itemCondition Condition grade (A, B, C, D, or F)

Condition Grading Scale

Standardized 5-tier condition assessment for secondhand products, ensuring consistent quality expectations across all platforms and markets.

CIRPASSX condition grading scale from A (excellent) to F (for parts)
Grade Name Description Condition
A Excellent Like new, minimal signs of use 90-100%
B Very Good Light wear, fully functional 75-90%
C Good Normal wear, fully functional 50-75%
D Fair Heavy wear, functional 25-50%
F For Parts Not fully functional, suitable for parts or repair <25%
